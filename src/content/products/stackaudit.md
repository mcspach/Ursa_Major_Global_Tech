StackAudit started as an internal tool: every white-label project and product
acquisition we touched needed the same question answered — *how healthy is this
codebase, really?* Now it's a product. Point it at a repository and get a
ranked, human-readable health report covering architecture, security, test
coverage, and the true cost of the debt hiding inside.

In beta with select acquisition clients. Ask us about early access.
